💬 Chat Process

Customer Service Consultant

🏠 Work From Home 🎓 Freshers Welcome
⏰ Working Hours: Mostly between 11:30 AM – 12:30 AM (including 1-hour break) · 9-hour availability required during training & certification
📋 Roles & Responsibilities
  • Resolving chats raised by customers through the app which requires coordination between pickup and delivery executives and stores
  • Resolve tickets for issues such as: Order status, Cancellation, Delivery instructions & Location-related issues
  • Provide inbound chat support by answering customer queries and making outbound calls to PDPs or stores related to their order
  • Establish relationship and trust with customers, PDPs and stores by ensuring qualitative service
  • Know the products inside and out to answer queries and resolve complaints
  • Process orders, forms, applications and requests
  • Keep records of customer interactions, transactions, comments and complaints
  • Maintain a positive, empathetic and professional attitude toward customers at all times
  • Ensure customer satisfaction and provide professional customer support via chats

📈 Sales & Marketing

Sales Executive

🚀 Growth Track
📋 Job Description
🎯 Executing Sales Strategy
  • Assist Zonal Head in translating the overall sales strategy / targets for zone to individual districts and regions
  • Communicate the sales targets in the form of Journey Cycle Plan to team of Sales Officers and oversee execution as per plan
  • Establish, measure timelines and mechanisms to regularly review the performance of team against the set targets and provide guidance and support to the team for identified issues
  • Coordinate with the Technical Services team to ensure highest standards of customer satisfaction
  • Support Zonal Head in performing periodic demand forecasting across districts; participate in sales and operations planning discussions and manage timely demand planning activities
  • Track competitor activities and provide data on competitor performance, price points, etc.
⚙️ Sales Operations
  • Oversee Sales Officers, assign their periodic targets, guide them in activity planning
  • Analyse sales performance data for own team of Sales Officers to ensure course correction wherever required
  • Ensure adherence to defined standard operating procedures to sell products and record sales
  • Ensure compliance of team to internal technology platforms for tracking sales performance, attendance, etc.
  • Collaborate with Technical services team in implementing the designed marketing activities
  • Ensure timely delivery of product in the assigned territory through co-ordination with logistics teams and local distributors
🤝 Dealer Network Management
  • Support expansion of dealer network to maximize market penetration & sales volumes
  • Drive and oversee channel partner activation through drives and campaigns
  • Oversee smooth and effective rollout of trade discounts, promotions and schemes in line with functional objectives
👥 People Management
  • Drive a performance driven culture — set goals, review performance and provide feedback so as to ensure a motivated and committed team
  • Develop employee capability and build functional excellence through various learning and development interventions and mentoring initiatives
  • Foster an environment of learning, excellence and innovation within the team and across teams
